Your family’s log

Where your information goes

Feeding times, amounts, durations, and notes are stored in the Google Sheet you choose. Anyone with access to that sheet can see its contents. Keep its sharing restricted to the people you intend.

Google handles sign-in and enforces sheet access. This app requests permission to use files you explicitly select through Google Picker. It does not request access to all your spreadsheets.

This browser remembers your OAuth client ID, restricted Picker API key, project number, selected sheet ID, and volume preference. Access tokens are kept in session storage for the current tab so a refresh can reconnect automatically until the token expires. Signing out or forgetting settings removes the saved token. Closing the tab normally ends this storage session; browser session restoration may retain it. Loaded records are kept only in memory. Unsaved form values are not deliberately stored by the app. Your browser may retain its own history or form state.

The service worker caches only the public app files. Records are not available after an offline restart. No analytics, advertising, or third-party error reporting is included.

GitHub serves the public app files; Google receives authentication and API requests under its own policies. Your records are sent directly from your browser to Google, not to GitHub. Browser client IDs and API keys are visible during use and must not be treated as secrets.

Removing information

Use “Forget this phone’s settings” to remove saved setup values. Signing out clears this app’s session, but does not sign you out of Google or revoke prior consent. Remove the app’s access in your Google Account permissions to revoke consent. Edit or delete records directly in the sheet; Google may retain revision history or trash according to its policies.
