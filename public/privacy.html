Your family’s log

Where your information goes

Feeding times, amounts, durations, and notes are stored in your Google Sheet. Keep its sharing restricted to the people you intend.

With Apps Script, this browser saves the deployment URL and access key in localStorage. The script runs with its owner's Google authorization and checks the key before reading or writing. Anyone with that key can access the log through the script, even without direct access to the sheet. Keep the key private and publish only trusted apps on this website's origin. Requests send the key in their body, not in the URL.

With Google sign-in, the app requests access only to files selected through Google Picker. This browser remembers the OAuth client ID, Picker key, project number, selected sheet ID, and volume preference. Google access tokens remain in sessionStorage until expiration or sign-out; browser session restoration may retain them.

Loaded records remain in memory. The service worker caches only public app files, never records or backend requests. Records are unavailable after an offline restart. The app includes no analytics, advertising, or remote error reporting. Google receives the backend requests; GitHub serves the app files.

Removing information

Use “Forget this phone’s settings” to remove saved setup, including its access key. To revoke a copied key, the script owner must replace ACCESS_KEY in Script Properties and update the authorized phones. Forgetting settings does not delete sheet records. In Google sign-in mode, signing out clears the app session; revoke consent through your Google Account permissions if needed. Edit or delete records in the sheet; Google may retain revision history or trash according to its policies.
